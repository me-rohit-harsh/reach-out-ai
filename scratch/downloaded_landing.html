FAQ

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Does it need my LinkedIn login?

No. ReachOut AI only uses publicly visible LinkedIn search result pages. You paste the URL, we scrape the public content. Your LinkedIn account is never touched.

How does the AI personalization work?

It reads the full post text, identifies the author's name and title, then matches your CV skills against what the post asks for. Every email references something specific — not a generic template.

Is there a risk of my email being marked as spam?

We send one email at a time with a 30–60 second random delay between each. This mimics human sending behaviour. We recommend using Gmail with an App Password for best results.

What is 'Test Mode'?

Test Mode scrapes all posts, collects emails, and shows you the full list — without sending a single message. Use it to verify quality before going live. Always recommended for first runs.
